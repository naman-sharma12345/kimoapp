# Phase 12 visual review

Inspected the actual first-second and one-frame-per-second sheets saved in
`baselines/phase12-reviews`. These are sparse visual checks, not full playback,
listening, lip-sync checks or proof of frame identity. Source credits accompany
each case. The fixtures use deterministic plans, not live provider output.

## TH-1 · shorts-clean

- The first image has the subject looking down with a hand across his face.
- That is a weak opening even though speech onset meets the numerical target.
- The portrait crop retains the head but leaves little breathing room.
- The hook text competes with captions during the first second.
- Several later caption blocks move onto the forehead.
- The black caption box is readable; the dim inactive words are less readable.
- The sampled facial colour remains recognizably natural, without a neon look.
- The long sequence remains mostly the same talking head.
- The score increase does not establish that a human would prefer this cut.
- Next: choose an eyes-up hook and fix face-aware caption placement first.

## TH-2 · shorts-punchy
