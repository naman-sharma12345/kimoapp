# Replacement raw dataset

The user explicitly requires new raw footage for final Phase 2 acceptance and has no footage to supply. The original version-1 set remains immutable legacy evidence. Six replacement selections are now prepared and hash-frozen in [prepared-manifest.json](prepared-manifest.json); actual selected-source reviews are in [REVIEW.md](REVIEW.md). Rejected candidates stay separate in candidates.json. Preparation and source review do not by themselves establish final renderer acceptance.

Each accepted source needs a stable URL, downloaded SHA256, licence/credit and exact selected interval. Check actual picture and sound for case fit, pre-existing cuts, captions, camera motion and repeated/static source frames. A licence declaration or the word “raw” in a title does not establish those properties. Do not split a finished montage into files and call it a raw vlog. Do not add synthetic speech or loop short clips to satisfy duration. Do not mark source ambiguity as an observed compositor pass.

The acquisition workflow checks candidates on standard hosted Windows runners. It preserves the original source bytes and published SHA1 where available, computes SHA256, probes metadata and exports small candidate contact sheets/first-second frames. A follow-up can reuse the exact artifact bytes, generate a small playable source preview and transcribe it with local Whisper on the runner. Acquisition is not a benchmark render, Creator Score comparison or AI replay. Missing cases and licence/content review remain explicit in `candidates.json`. The initial observations and a rejected source are recorded in [REVIEW.md](REVIEW.md).

The existing cumulative USD 5.49 reservation stays unchanged. Acquiring licensed footage does not authorize any new paid AI calls. Final editing baselines, matched before/after runs and honest 10–20-line critiques remain required after the dataset is frozen. Keep large originals, prepared files and render outputs outside Git and outside this PC's OneDrive checkout.
