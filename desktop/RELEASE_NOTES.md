kimo for Windows 0.2.0: watch every edit happen live, YouTube-grade masters, and a timeline you can edit yourself.

**Live, every step**
- Every AI thought, plan and kimo tool call streams as it happens, with a running timer on each call and its result or error
- The preview updates after every visual change (cuts, titles, captions, grade, punch-ins), with a smooth crossfade
- A visual timeline fills in as clips, titles and captions are added
- Render progress with % and ETA (kimo's render time is learned on your PC; the upload master shows exact progress)

**YouTube-quality output**
- Style presets steer the edit plan: Energetic (MrBeast-style), Documentary, Podcast clip, Cinematic, Tutorial, Auto
- Expert edit planning: hook in the first 2–3 s, pauses and filler segments cut, jump-cut punch-ins, keyword-highlighted captions inside the safe area, title cards, colour grade
- YouTube upload master: H.264 High, closed GOP of half the frame rate, 2 B-frames, BT.709, YouTube's recommended bitrate, AAC-LC 384 kbps 48 kHz, faststart
- Loudness measured (EBU R128) and pulled to -14 LUFS with true peak at -1.5 dBTP or lower
- Stronger frame check: 16 sampled frames + 2 full-size frames per pass, plus measured checks for black frames, dead air in the hook, long pauses and lost sound; fixes and re-renders up to 3 passes
- Quality tab with resolution, fps, bitrate, LUFS, true peak and every check

**New**
- Drag-to-edit timeline: move/reorder, trim either edge, delete, double-click a caption to fix its words, then Apply & re-render through kimo (with undo)
- Ask kimo for changes in chat ("make the hook punchier"): applied on top of the current edit, not from scratch
- YouTube tab: auto thumbnail with big text, title, description and chapters (when the video is long enough), one-click copy
- Before / after toggle (B)
- Keyboard: Space, J/K/L, ←/→ frame step, B, Delete, Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Enter, Ctrl+S, / to ask, 1/2/3 tabs, ? for the list
- Follows your Windows light or dark mode

**Fixed**
- Captions-only mode could drop the clip's original sound
