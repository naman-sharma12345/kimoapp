# Audio

Bad audio loses viewers faster than bad video.

- Dialogue is king: voice clips at volume 1.0. Music under speech at 0.12 to 0.25 with duckUnderVoice true; music alone (intros, montages) 0.6 to 0.9.
- Fade music in 0.5 to 1 s and out 1 to 2 s; never hard-stop music unless it lands on a deliberate cut to silence.
- Cut music to end on a phrase or beat, not mid-note. Use beats[] when present.
- J-cuts and L-cuts in interviews: let the next speaker's audio start 0.2 to 0.5 s before their picture (or the previous audio trail under the next shot). Achieve by splitting audio onto its own track and offsetting.
- Every visual cut that needs energy can carry a whoosh or hit SFX, but only if an SFX asset exists. Never invent one.
- Moments of silence (0.3 to 0.8 s) before a punchline make it hit harder; the one place silence is good in a short.
- Remove audio from B-roll by default (volume 0) unless its natural sound adds atmosphere (then 0.1 to 0.3).
