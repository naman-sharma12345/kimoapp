# Captions and on-screen text

## Captions

- Use transcript words[] timings. Build caption clips on a dedicated text track, each 1 to 4 words (shorts) or one short line (long-form, max ~42 characters).
- Each caption clip starts at its first word's t and ends at the last word's t + d (or the next caption's start, whichever is first). No gaps flicker: if the gap is under 0.15 s, extend.
- Lowercase or sentence case reads calmer; ALL CAPS (caption-pop) reads louder. Match the energy of the video.
- Highlight one key word per line by giving it its own clip with a contrasting color (e.g. #FFD400 or the brand color) and pop words animation. Do this sparingly, about once per sentence at most.
- Remove filler from captions even if a little remains in audio. Fix obvious transcription errors only when certain.
- Never place captions over faces or important action. In vertical, keep them between 55% and 80% of the frame height.

## Titles and callouts

- One idea per text element, under 6 words when possible.
- On screen long enough to read twice: about 0.3 s per word, minimum 1.2 s.
- Animate in (rise or fade, 0.2 to 0.3 s), cut out cleanly or fade 0.15 s.
- Hierarchy: one title style, one caption style, one label style per video. Don't mix more than two font families.
- Contrast: white text gets the outlined/boxed style over busy footage; dark text only on light, calm backgrounds.
