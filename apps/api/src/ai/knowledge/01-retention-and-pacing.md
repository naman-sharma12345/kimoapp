# Retention and pacing

The audience decides in the first 1 to 3 seconds. Every cut either earns the next second or loses the viewer.

## The hook (0 to 3 s)

- Open on the most interesting moment, not the beginning. If the best line is at 2:14, cold-open with it (3 to 6 s), then cut to the start.
- No logos, no "hey guys", no slow fade from black at the start. First frame must already be moving or speaking.
- Put a title or bold caption on screen in frame 1 stating the payoff ("i edited this in 4 minutes").
- Start mid-sentence or mid-action when possible. Trim pre-roll breath and setup to zero.

## Cutting speech (jump cuts)

- Remove silences longer than ~0.25 s in shorts and ~0.4 s in long-form. Leave 2 to 4 frames of air before and after words so cuts don't clip consonants.
- Remove ums, uhs, restarts, repeated phrases and "so, basically". Keep the take where the sentence lands cleanly, usually the last attempt.
- Hide jump cuts by alternating scale: 1.0 then 1.12 to 1.2 punch-in on the next segment (transform.scale, re-centre x/y on the face). Don't punch in on every cut; every 2nd or 3rd, plus on key lines.

## Cadence

- Shorts / Reels / TikTok: a visual change every 1 to 3 s (cut, punch, B-roll, text pop). Total 20 to 45 s unless the user asks otherwise.
- YouTube long-form: a visual change every 4 to 8 s, a stronger pattern interrupt (B-roll, graphic, music shift, angle change) every 30 to 60 s.
- Podcast clips: 30 to 90 s, one idea, starts on the strongest sentence, ends on the punchline. Cut the question if the answer stands alone.
- Montage / music edits: cut on beats (assets may carry beats[]). Downbeats for big moments, every beat or half-beat for fast sections, let shots breathe in quiet parts.

## Structure

- Hook, then context in one line, then payoff in order of rising value, then a fast ending. No long outros. End on the strongest line or a loopable moment (for shorts, make the last frame flow back into the first).
- Open loops: tease a result early ("the last one surprised me"), pay it off late.
- Remove anything that doesn't serve the one idea. Shorter beats longer.

## Speed

- Speed ramps: 1.0 into 2 to 4x through boring travel, back to 1.0 on the moment. Speech stays 1.0 to 1.15 with keepPitch true.
- Slow motion (0.5) only for footage likely shot at high frame rate, and only on the hero moment.
